Questions about learning, training and Ethan platforms.

Quick answers to common enquiries before you contact the Academy.

Who can learn with Ethan Digital Academy?

Our learning activities can serve children, students, professionals, business owners, schools and organisations depending on the programme.

How do I choose a programme?

Start with your age or role, current skill level and goal. You can also contact the Academy for guidance.

Are all Ethan platforms the same website?

No. The ecosystem contains specialised platforms for different tasks, including learning, coding, AI, productivity and children’s digital education.

Can an individual, group or organisation request custom training?

Yes. Training can be discussed around your objectives, audience, skill level, preferred topics and schedule.

How do I request a digital service?

Use the enquiry form or WhatsApp contact and describe the outcome you need. The Academy can then discuss scope and next steps.
