Founder’s Corner

Meet the founder, understand the vision and discover the experience behind Ethan Digital Academy.

About the Founder

Mr. Abayomi Peter Oyenuga

B.Sc. Accounting (Second Class Upper, 2:1), Babcock University • MBA

Mr. Abayomi Peter Oyenuga is the Founder of Ethan Digital Academy, bringing over two decades of professional experience across diverse sectors and areas of responsibility. His experience spans Business Management, Finance, Banking, Insurance, Sales, Marketing, Business Development, Digital Technology, Digital Marketing, Website Development & Maintenance, Leadership and Strategic Management.

His multidisciplinary experience, combined with his growing involvement in technology and digital education, informs Ethan Digital Academy’s practical approach to digital skills development, professional training, innovation and technology-enabled growth.

He is married to Mrs. Adebosola Oyenuga (MBA), and they are blessed with children.

Introduction

Ethan Digital Academy is built around a simple idea: technology should be understandable, practical and useful. The Academy seeks to help learners and organisations build relevant knowledge, develop practical capability and grow confidently in an increasingly digital world.

Founder’s Vision

A learning environment where technology empowers rather than intimidates.

“My vision is to build Ethan Digital Academy into a learning environment where people are not intimidated by technology, but are empowered to understand it, use it and grow with it. We want every learner—young or old—to have the opportunity to acquire relevant knowledge, develop practical skills, embrace innovation and become better prepared for the opportunities and challenges of a digital future.”

The vision guides Ethan Digital Academy’s commitment to relevant knowledge, practical skills, innovation and responsible digital participation.
