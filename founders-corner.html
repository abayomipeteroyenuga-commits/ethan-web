Founder’s Corner

Mr. Abayomi Peter Oyenuga

Founder, Ethan Digital Academy

A Forward-Looking Vision

Ethan Digital Academy was founded from a clear and forward-looking vision: to make relevant digital knowledge, technology education, professional development and future-ready skills accessible to people of different ages and backgrounds, while creating an environment where learning leads to practical capability, innovation, personal development and meaningful growth.

The vision goes beyond teaching people how to use technology. It is about building confident learners, digitally empowered individuals, skilled professionals, innovative young minds, entrepreneurs and future leaders.

Mission

The mission of Ethan Digital Academy is to provide accessible, practical and high-quality education in technology, digital skills, artificial intelligence, coding, cybersecurity, business, entrepreneurship and professional development. Through structured learning, modern educational technology, practical instruction and continuous skills development, the Academy seeks to bridge the gap between knowledge and practical application.

About the Founder

Mr. Abayomi Peter Oyenuga is an experienced professional, educator, entrepreneur and leader with a multidisciplinary career spanning more than two decades across diverse areas of business and professional practice.

He holds a Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) degree in Accounting from Babcock University, graduating with Second Class Upper Division (2:1), as well as a Master of Business Administration (MBA).

His professional experience spans Business Management, Finance, Banking, Insurance, Sales, Marketing, Business Development, Digital Technology, Digital Marketing, Website Development & Maintenance, Leadership, and Strategic Management. This diverse background provides him with a broad perspective on business, people, leadership, technology, organisational development, and the evolving demands of the modern economy.

His experience across traditional business disciplines and emerging digital technologies has strengthened his conviction that technology and digital competence should not be limited to technology professionals alone. Children, students, professionals, entrepreneurs, business owners, schools and organisations all need opportunities to understand technology, develop relevant skills and confidently participate in today's digital world.

This conviction inspired the development of Ethan Digital Academy, bringing together education, technology, digital learning and professional development under a unified vision.

Beyond his professional, educational and entrepreneurial pursuits, Mr. Abayomi Peter Oyenuga is happily married to Mrs. Adebosola Oyenuga (MBA), and their marriage is blessed with children.

“My vision is to build Ethan Digital Academy into a learning environment where people are not intimidated by technology, but are empowered to understand it, use it and grow with it. We want every learner—young or old—to have the opportunity to acquire relevant knowledge, develop practical skills, embrace innovation and become better prepared for the opportunities and challenges of a digital future.”

— Mr. Abayomi Peter Oyenuga
